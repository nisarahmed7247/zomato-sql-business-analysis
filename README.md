# 🍽️ Zomato SQL Business Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Zomato's customer, restaurant, and order data using **MySQL** to generate business-focused insights across revenue performance, customer behavior, restaurant and cuisine performance, discounts, order economics, and business growth.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how SQL can be used not only for querying data, but also to solve **practical business problems and support data-driven decision-making**.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset consists of three main tables:

### `customers`

Contains customer-level information:

* Customer ID
* Customer Name
* City
* Signup Time
* Acquisition Channel

### `restaurants`

Contains restaurant-level information:

* Restaurant ID
* Restaurant Name
* Cuisine
* City
* Average Rating

### `orders`

Contains transaction-level information:

* Order ID
* Customer ID
* Restaurant ID
* Order Timestamp
* Order Amount
* Discount Amount
* Delivery Fee
* Payment Mode
* Order Status

# 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis consists of **25 business questions** divided into five analytical areas.

---

# 1. Revenue & Business Performance

### Q1. Revenue Trend

Calculate total delivered revenue and monthly revenue trend.

### Q2. City Performance

For each city, calculate:

* Total delivered revenue
* AOV
* Active customers
* Revenue contribution

Then identify cities with **above-average active customers but below-average revenue contribution**.

### Q3. Top Restaurants by City

Within each city, identify the **top 3 restaurants by delivered revenue**.

For each selected restaurant, show:

* City
* Revenue
* Number of delivered orders
* AOV
* Percentage contribution to the city's revenue

### Q4. Month-over-Month Revenue Growth

Calculate **month-over-month revenue growth** for delivered orders.

For each month, show:

* Monthly revenue
* Previous month revenue
* MoM growth percentage

### Q5. Revenue Concentration

Identify the restaurants that collectively generate the **first 80% of total delivered revenue**.

For each restaurant, show:

* Revenue
* Revenue contribution %
* Cumulative revenue contribution %

---

# 2. Customer Behaviour & Value

### Q6. Top 10 Customers

Identify the **top 10 customers by total delivered spending**.

Calculate:

* Total spending
* Delivered orders
* AOV
* Revenue contribution to total delivered revenue

### Q7. Customer Value Segmentation

Segment customers into **Low, Medium, and High-value groups** based on total delivered spending using thresholds derived from average customer spending.

Calculate:

* Number of customers
* Total revenue
* Average spending
* Revenue contribution

### Q8. Repeat vs One-Time Customers

Compare repeat customers with one-time customers.

For each group, calculate:

* Number of customers
* Total delivered revenue
* Average customer spending
* Average delivered orders per customer
* Revenue contribution

### Q9. Customer Acquisition Channel Analysis

For each customer acquisition channel, among customers who have placed at least one delivered order, calculate:

* Active customers
* Repeat-customer rate
* Average customer spending
* Average delivered orders per customer

Compare channels to understand differences in **customer retention and engagement**.

### Q10. Customer Revenue Concentration

Identify what percentage of total delivered revenue is generated by the:

* Top 10% of customers
* Top 20% of customers
* Top 30% of customers

Customers are ranked based on spending.

---

# 3. Restaurant & Cuisine Performance

### Q11. Restaurant Performance vs City Benchmark

For each restaurant, compare its:

* Delivered order volume
* Delivered revenue

against the average restaurant in the same city.

Identify restaurants with:

* **Above-city-average order volume**
* **Below-city-average revenue**

### Q12. Cuisine Revenue & Rating Performance

For each cuisine, calculate:

* Total delivered orders
* Total delivered revenue
* AOV
* Average restaurant rating

Then identify cuisines whose:

* Revenue is **above the overall average cuisine revenue**
* Average restaurant rating is **below the overall average cuisine rating**

### Q13. Restaurant Revenue vs Rating

Identify restaurants whose delivered revenue is **above the average revenue of restaurants in their city**, but whose average restaurant rating is **below the average rating of restaurants in their city**.

For each restaurant, show:

* Revenue
* City-average revenue
* Restaurant rating
* City-average rating

### Q14. Restaurant Customer Repeat Behaviour

For each restaurant, calculate:

* Number of unique customers who placed delivered orders
* Total delivered orders
* Average orders per customer

Then identify restaurants where average orders per customer are **above the overall average across restaurants**.

### Q15. Cuisine Demand & AOV

For each cuisine, calculate:

* Total delivered orders
* Total delivered revenue
* Unique customers who placed delivered orders
* AOV

Then identify cuisines with:

* **Above-average delivered order volume**
* **Below-average AOV**

# 4. Orders, Discounts & Unit Economics

### Q16. Discount Band Analysis

Create the following discount bands:

* **No Discount:** ₹0
* **Low Discount:** >₹0–₹50
* **Medium Discount:** >₹50–₹100
* **High Discount:** >₹100

For each discount band, calculate:

* Delivered orders
* Revenue
* Total discount
* Average discount per order
* AOV

Then identify bands with **above-average discount per order but below-average AOV across discount bands**.

### Q17. Order Status Analysis

For each order status, calculate:

* Number of orders
* Total order value
* Average order value
* Average discount amount
* Order volume contribution
* Order value contribution

### Q18. City Order Status Analysis

For each city, calculate:

* Total orders
* Delivered orders
* Cancelled orders
* Refunded orders
* Cancellation rate
* Refund rate

Identify cities with **both cancellation and refund rates above their respective overall city averages**.

### Q19. Delivery Fee Band Analysis

Create the following delivery-fee bands:

* **Free:** ₹0
* **Low:** >₹0–₹30
* **Medium:** >₹30–₹60
* **High:** >₹60

For each delivery-fee band, calculate:

* Delivered orders
* Delivered revenue
* Average delivery fee
* AOV

Identify bands with **above-average delivery fee but below-average AOV**.

### Q20. Payment Mode Performance

For each payment mode, calculate:

* Delivered orders
* Delivered revenue
* AOV
* Revenue contribution

Identify payment modes with **above-average AOV but below-average revenue contribution**.

# 5. Growth, Trends & Strategic Insights

### Q21. Monthly Growth Drivers

Compare **2024 vs 2025** monthly:

* Delivered orders
* Delivered revenue
* Order growth %
* Revenue growth %

Identify months where **revenue increased but orders declined**.

### Q22. City Year-over-Year Growth

For each city, compare **2024 vs 2025**:

* Delivered revenue
* Delivered orders
* Revenue growth %
* Order growth %

Identify cities where **revenue grew but orders declined**.

### Q23. City Customer Growth

For each city, calculate **2024 and 2025**:

* Active customers
* Delivered revenue
* Active customer growth %
* Revenue growth %

Identify cities where **revenue increased but active customers declined**.

### Q24. Restaurant Revenue Growth

For each restaurant, compare **2024 vs 2025 delivered revenue** and calculate revenue growth %.

Identify restaurants with **more than 20% revenue decline in 2025**.

### Q25. Cuisine Revenue Growth

For each cuisine, compare **2024 vs 2025 delivered revenue** and calculate growth %.

Identify cuisines with **growth above 10%** and rank them by growth.
